        signature entry with TWO signerInfos: signerA (Witness AG, a legal person other than

why it fails without the fix: MEASURED. In the pre-fix copy of the verifier, this container
        reports exit 0 CONFORMANT and prints "checked: the signer's public key differs from the
        operator's" and "p3.6 operator exclusion checked against --operator-cert" — a report that
        asserts the exclusion was checked while the operator co-signed. The pre-fix tool kept
        only the FIRST certificate of openssl's -signer output, so signerA was "the signer" and
        the operator was never read. The control reports exit 0 both before and after, which is
        what makes this a fix and not a tightening.
openssl: the signer set is enumerated from `openssl cms -verify ... -signer signers.pem`, which
        writes one certificate per signerInfo, and held against the count of signerInfos in
        `openssl cms -cmsout -inform DER -in sig.p7s -print`:
          $ openssl cms -verify -noverify -binary -inform DER -in signatures.p7s \
              -content META-INF/manifest.xml -purpose any -out /dev/null -signer signers.pem
          signers.pem: 2 certificates
            1. subject=CN=Witness AG, O=martelage test PKI (throwaway)
            2. subject=CN=Operator GmbH, O=martelage test PKI (throwaway)
          $ openssl cms -cmsout -inform DER -in signatures.p7s -print | grep -c issuerAndSerialNumber
          2
        If those two numbers ever disagree the run REFUSES rather than reporting the signers it
        happened to see: an enumeration that cannot be checked is not an enumeration.
