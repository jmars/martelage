how the dates are fixed, and why this needed one more step than the CRLs: `openssl ocsp`
        timestamps a response with the build second (thisUpdate = now, nextUpdate = now +
        -ndays), and it refuses a non-positive -ndays/-nmin ("Non-positive number", MEASURED on
        openssl 3.5.3), so a response generated today can never be expired tomorrow and its
        window cannot be moved backwards from the command line. tools/mkocsp.sh therefore moves
        the two GeneralizedTime fields of every shipped response to the same fixed dates the
        certificates and CRLs use — 2020-01-01 for thisUpdate, 2099-01-01 for nextUpdate, and
        2021-01-01 here — and re-signs the moved tbsResponseData with the same responder key. The
        result is a response the responder key genuinely signed; what is fixed is its window
