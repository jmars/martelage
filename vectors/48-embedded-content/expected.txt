        wording change. On the pre-fix verifier this container reports exit 0

        The same containers, one flag apart, measured on this tree:

what this vector does NOT claim: that a container with embedded content is evidence of
        nothing. Its seal covers real bytes and the pre-fix tool verified them. What is
        claimed is narrower and is the whole of what the tool can establish: the
        construction is not the one this verifier implements, so no signature, digest,
        chain or date is reported as checked for it, and the claim that this container
        is the P3 evidence is refused rather than left undecided. ETSI EN 319 162-1's
        multi-file concatenated-digest form and XAdES are equally unimplemented, and
        this refusal names them for the same reason
