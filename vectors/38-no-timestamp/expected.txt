vector: 38-no-timestamp
what:   a sealed container with no RFC 3161 token, and a claim whose
        claim.timestamp is "none" — so the container does not contradict its own
        claim. Two different statements follow, and they must not be the same one:
        --require-timestamp makes the absence a refusal, and without it the absence
        leaves the certificate-date check unreachable, because no verified time can
        be established to check the dates against
run:    validator/p3-verify --container vectors/38-no-timestamp/seal.asice \
          --require-timestamp --trust-anchors vectors/pki/anchors \
          --operator-cert vectors/pki/operator.crt.pem \
          vectors/38-no-timestamp/pkg
exit:   1
also:   validator/p3-verify --container vectors/38-no-timestamp/seal.asice \
          --trust-anchors vectors/pki/anchors \
          --operator-cert vectors/pki/operator.crt.pem \
          vectors/38-no-timestamp/pkg   ->  exit 3
        no --require-timestamp: nothing is refused, and the date check cannot be
        reached. The two arms differ by one flag
must print:
  - "--require-timestamp was given and the container holds no RFC 3161 token"
requirements exercised: MA-64, MA-69, MA-70
